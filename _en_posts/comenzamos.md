## Tourist Trap in under two minutes

An elevator pitch is about presenting your project in the time it takes to ride a lift. We filmed ours in front of a green screen so we could show gameplay in the background! While we talk, you can see the beach bar, the kitchen and the beach in action behind us.

<video class="video-vertical" controls playsinline preload="none" poster="/assets/blog/blog-0/elevator-pitch-poster-en.jpg" src="/assets/blog/blog-0/elevator-pitch-en.mp4"></video>

*The Tourist Trap elevator pitch, narrated in Spanish with English subtitles.*

In the video we cover the essentials:

- **What it is:** A cooking tycoon sim set in an Andalusian beach bar, with different minigames for preparing dishes.
- **The story:** Curro, our grumpy and lazy hero, gets a “scratch and win” card that turns out to be a “scratch and pay”. The prize is a beach bar in ruins and a debt with Rascacienda (our take on the tax office), and the only help comes from his uncle, Rogelio Chiringelio.
- **The humour:** Inspired by Francisco Ibáñez and his comic *Mortadelo y Filemón* (known in English as *Mort & Phil*), with chaotic events like fires or tsunamis in the middle of service.
- **The plan:** We want to release it on Steam in May 2028, and we're looking for funding to finish it, grow the team and bring it to more platforms.

> Important: remember the gameplay comes from a VERY early build of the game. We're still in development!
